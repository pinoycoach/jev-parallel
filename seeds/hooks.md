# Hook patterns (structure only)

One pattern per line, starting with "- ". The generator keeps the structure and writes fresh wording.
Never paste someone's copy here to be reused word for word. Describe the skeleton.

These ten skeletons were drawn by Claude from the hooks Nap approved in calls/calls.md (12 Nov 2026).
They are not trends observed in the wild. Approved hooks that made a health or guaranteed-result
claim were left out as seeds.

- The secret an authority supposedly hides: "The [big word] [fix] that [experts] don't want you to know about"
- Unlock promise: "Unlock your best [benefit] ever with our [strong adjective] [thing]"
- Someone else's reaction with a punchline: "My [professional] asked what changed. I said [product]. They [reaction]"
- Ad that admits it is an ad: "This is not an ad. Okay, it is. But [why I'd still say it]"
- Curiosity tease about a dose or step: "You won't believe what happened when I [did slightly more]"
- Small list of changes: "Three things I stopped doing after I [result]"
- Flat superlative: "The only [product type] that actually works"
- The reversal: "I returned it. Then I ordered it again"
- Grudging convert: "I expected nothing and I'm still a little annoyed it worked"
- Before and after in a voice: "What my [bad moment] used to sound like, and what it sounds like now"
