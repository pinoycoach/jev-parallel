# Hook patterns (structure only)

One pattern per line, starting with "- ". The generator keeps the structure and writes fresh wording.
Never paste someone's copy here to be reused word for word. Describe the skeleton.

These ten skeletons were drawn by Claude from the hooks Nap approved in calls/calls.md (12 Nov 2026).
They are not trends observed in the wild.

- Long struggle, then someone hands it over: "I [struggled] for [time]. Then my [person] left this on my desk"
- Day count with a late payoff: "Day 1: nothing. Day [N]: [small proof it worked]"
- Someone mistakes it for something else: "My [person] thought it was [other thing]. [What happened next]"
- Job as proof: "I'm a [job]. This is the only thing in my bag that isn't [staple]"
- The honest downside: "Nobody tells you the first [period] [is unpleasant in this way]"
- Proved wrong: "I bought this to prove my [person] wrong. They were right"
- Cheap versus expensive test: "I tried the cheap one and the expensive one for [time] each. Here's the difference"
- Call-out by pain: "Stop scrolling if you [felt this today]"
- Age or assumption overturned: "At [age] I thought [problem] was just [cause]"
- Side effect on someone else: "My [pet or person] now [new routine]. They're confused. So am I"
