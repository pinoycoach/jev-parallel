# Sign-off calls

Format, one per line:
    `- APPROVE | the hook | why`
    `- REJECT | the hook | why`

NAP: these are stand-ins so the wiring runs. They are NOT your taste. Replace every [PLACEHOLDER]
line with your own 20 to 30 calls. The "why" matters most: it is what the skill file is built from.
Calls you make in the inbox are appended to the bottom of this file.

- APPROVE | [PLACEHOLDER] I nearly sent this back, then my sister borrowed it and never returned it | specific person, small true-sounding moment
- APPROVE | [PLACEHOLDER] Day one I hated it, day thirty I bought a second one | clear turn, no hype words
- APPROVE | [PLACEHOLDER] Nobody tells you the first week with this is annoying | honest downside earns trust
- APPROVE | [PLACEHOLDER] My mother laughed at the price until she tried it | a real reaction, plain speech
- APPROVE | [PLACEHOLDER] I tested the cheap one for a month so you do not have to | concrete effort, useful
- REJECT | [PLACEHOLDER] This revolutionary product will change your life forever | hype, says nothing
- REJECT | [PLACEHOLDER] Doctors hate this one simple trick | clickbait and an unprovable claim
- REJECT | [PLACEHOLDER] Unlock your best self with our game-changing solution | brochure voice
- REJECT | [PLACEHOLDER] You will not believe what happens next | empty tease
- REJECT | [PLACEHOLDER] Guaranteed results in just three days or less | a promise we cannot keep
